# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Treat people respectfully in issues, pull requests, reviews and
other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback, assume questions may be sincere, and respect others'
  time and boundaries.
- Accept that a proposal may be declined and that maintainers may need time to
  respond.
- Protect other people's privacy and obtain permission before sharing their
  messages, personal information or document data.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing,
targeted intimidation and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a concern that can safely be described publicly, notify a repository
maintainer without repeating private or harmful material. If a private report is
needed, ask a maintainer to arrange a private channel; do not publish identifying
details while doing so. GitHub's reporting tools are also available for conduct
that violates its platform policies. Security vulnerabilities follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, warn
participants, or restrict participation, taking the context and severity into
account. They will aim to explain decisions when doing so is safe. This is a
small volunteer project, not a promise of immediate moderation or confidentiality
beyond the reporting channel's actual protections.
